Governance note. This is a proposed portfolio decision-support framework, not an official GSMA scoring model. The 80% benchmark and score weights are project assumptions. “Population represented by gap” is an analytical estimate derived from population × benchmark gap and must not be presented as a count of unique offline people. Mobile subscriptions are contextual and are not network coverage. Indicator-year visibility and confidence ratings should remain visible in any production Power BI version.
