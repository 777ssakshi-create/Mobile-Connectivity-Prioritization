# Assumptions and Limitations

## Portfolio assumptions

- The 80% Internet-usage benchmark is a project assumption.
- Priority-score weights are proposed analytical weights, not stakeholder-approved organisational policy.
- Priority bands are presentation thresholds rather than validated intervention thresholds.
- Diagnostic segments are screening categories for investigation.

## Data limitations

- Indicator years can differ by market and by metric.
- Latest-available data improve recency but do not create perfect cross-country comparability.
- GDP per capita is used only as a simple economic vulnerability proxy.
- Rural population share is contextual and does not directly measure infrastructure availability.
- Mobile subscriptions per 100 people are not network-coverage measures and can exceed 100 because people may hold multiple subscriptions.
- Public aggregate records must be excluded before market-level percentile rankings are calculated.

## Population-impact interpretation

The estimated population impact is calculated from the benchmark gap and total population. It should not be described as the number of unique offline people.

## Causality

The correlation analysis and diagnostic segmentation describe patterns and screening signals. They do not establish causal relationships.

## Organisational disclosure

This is an independent Business Analyst portfolio case study using public data. Any GSMA-style workflow, prioritisation framework, stakeholder requirement, user story, target, score weight, process redesign or UAT artefact in the project is illustrative and was not elicited from internal GSMA stakeholders unless separately evidenced.
